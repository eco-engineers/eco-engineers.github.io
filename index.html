🌊 Project Overview

LEGO SPIKE Essential detects water quality indicators. OCR reads the sensor results and sends them to Firebase in real time. This dashboard analyzes the incoming data and shows the water quality status, helping identify harmful bacteria in water samples.

  1. 1Robot senses the sample color
  2. 2OCR reads the result
  3. 3Firebase stores it live
  4. 4Dashboard shows the status

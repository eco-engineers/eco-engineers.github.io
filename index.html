Explore water quality

Why clean water matters, how it is measured, and what we can do about it.

Did you know?

  • About 71% of Earth is covered by water.
  • Only about 1% of Earth's water is fresh water that people can easily use.
  • Warm, nutrient-rich water can grow cyanobacteria (blue-green algae), and some kinds make toxins.

Is the water healthy? Check the pH

pH tells us how acidic or basic water is. Drinking water is usually kept between 6.5 and 8.5.

0 acid7 neutral14 base

Other clues: cloudy water (turbidity), temperature, and color.

How we can protect water

  • Never pour paint, oil, or chemicals down a drain.
  • Use less fertilizer. Rain washes it into streams and feeds algae blooms.
  • Pick up litter near rivers, ponds, and storm drains.
  • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth.

Ideas to try next

  • Compare tap, rain, pond, and filtered water.
  • Build a filter from sand, gravel, and charcoal and measure how clear the water gets.
  • Add more sensors to the robot for pH, temperature, and cloudiness.
  • Send a text or email alert when a harmful bloom is detected.
